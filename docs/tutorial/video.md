# Videos

Two narrated films. The first explains the science of the reduction and what each step of the
program does about it; the second is the hands-on tutorial, NGC 134 reduced from scratch in the
interface. Both have subtitles (CC) you can switch on.

## The science and the steps

What TripleSpec records, what the atmosphere and the night sky do to it, why targets are nodded,
and then each step of the reduction — flat field, wavelength calibration, extraction, combining,
telluric correction, merging — with this night's own data and the interface (7 minutes).

## Hands-on: NGC 134 from scratch

The interface, recorded as it reduces one galaxy: choosing the folders, reading the night and
checking its plan, then every step in turn, every choice explained as it is made. Computations
play in real time while the narration is about them; the rest of a long wait is shown faster,
with a badge saying by how much (9 minutes).
